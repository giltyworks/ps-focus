## What's new

Changes since **1.1.27**:

- **Share a session as a story.** Click a day in the calendar, then **Share as story**, to make a picture of that session to post. Pick a photo to go behind it, and the card shows the date, the programs, your time, when you started and any records you set. Copy it, save it as a PNG, or save it into your iCloud Photos or Google Drive folder (this needs iCloud for Windows or Google Drive for desktop).
- **Rounder pop-ups.** The day and friend pop-ups have rounded corners on Windows 11 and Windows' own style of close button, and you can drag them by their title.

## Install

Download **PS-Focus-Setup-1.1.28.exe** below and run it. It updates an existing install and keeps your history, settings and Google sign-in.

The app isn't code-signed yet, so you may see two warnings:

- If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, keep it. In Edge, choose **⋯**, then **Keep**, then **Keep anyway**.
- If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
