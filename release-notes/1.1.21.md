## What's new

Changes since **1.1.0**:

- **PhotoCraft tracking.** PhotoCraft joins Photoshop, Krita and Clip Studio Paint, with separate history for each program.
- **One Today panel.** Automatically follows the supported program in front and keeps showing the last one when you switch away. Its dropdown lets you view another program's history without changing what gets tracked.
- **Friends.** Add people by friend code and see their levels, recent hours, today's activity and the program they're using. Friend requests and their notifications live inside the Friends panel, and longer lists scroll.
- **Presence controls.** Choose Online, Away, Invisible or Offline. While you're Online and tracking, your status shows the active program and your name turns light green; returning to PS Focus restores the blue Online display.
- **Organise your friends.** Favourites, nicknames, and separate In App, Online and Offline sections make the list easier to scan. Offline friends show when they were last online.
- **Friend details.** Double-click a friend's name strip to open their recent hours, lifetime total and membership date. These details no longer crowd a hover tooltip.
- **Smoother docking and dragging.** Panels slide along snapped edges with less jumping. Blue drag highlights work across modules, undocking preserves the point you grabbed, and connected panels move together. Detached panels can overlap freely without unexpectedly exchanging positions.
- **A more compact layout.** Narrower portrait layout, aligned landscape columns, module icons and reorganised Settings controls. Always on top is available from the main title bar's right-click menu.
- **Better Windows behaviour.** A custom title bar keeps the app's appearance consistent while retaining Windows snapping, Win + arrow shortcuts and taskbar restore behaviour.
- **Clearer transparency.** Panel backgrounds can fade while text stays solid, with a subtle glow for readability. Connected panels behave as one group when you move the pointer between them.
- **Consistent controls and readouts.** Matching dropdown arrows, button sizes and hand cursors; calendar hover details; clearer minute-based Stats values. Today shows PAUSED only when you manually pause, without redundant active/inactive labels.
- **More responsive, reliable tracking.** Activity status checks every 100ms. Fractional time is preserved, intervals split correctly across midnight, sleep and locked desktops are excluded, and temporary database failures retain time for retry. Orderly shutdown flushes pending time, with recovery on the next launch if saving temporarily fails.
- **An interactive website demo.** Try moving, undocking and snapping panels, explore sample friends and Settings, and simulate switching between PS Focus and the painting. The demo replaces the earlier reel and uses made-up data.
- **Code cleanup.** Removed redundant checks, unnecessary repaints and unused code, with expanded regression coverage.

Tracking samples foreground activity every 100ms. Long UI stalls can still undercount time; an independent sampler remains a follow-up.
