## What's new

Changes since **1.1.28**:

- **Reveal your level with a click.** Once feedback unlocks, click the blurred level to show it. The feedback form still opens, but sending feedback is now up to you. The level also shows in the title of the Stats module, as in `Level 7 / 99`.
- **Lighter on your PC.** PS Focus uses about half the processor time while it waits, and gives its memory back when it's hidden in the tray.
- **Story card.** A new slider over the photo lets you darken it, the picture starts a little darker than before, and the card's fade is subtler.
- **Friends and Google.** If you choose Offline, PS Focus now checks in once a day so your friends and code aren't deleted after six months away (see the privacy policy). A Google account that couldn't be read at start-up is retried, sign-in no longer hangs on a stray browser connection, and two Drive backups left by PCs that first synced at the same moment are merged into one.
- **Fixes.** The calendar moves on to the new month if PS Focus is left running past the 1st, modules no longer jump when you pull one off the top or left of another, and the transparency slider no longer shows a box behind it.

## Install

Download **PS-Focus-Setup-1.1.29.exe** below and run it. It updates an existing install and keeps your history, settings and Google sign-in.

The app isn't code-signed yet, so you may see two warnings:

- If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, keep it. In Edge, choose **⋯**, then **Keep**, then **Keep anyway**.
- If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
