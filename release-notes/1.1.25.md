## What's new

Changes since **1.1.21**:

- **No more frozen-looking window.** After a day's rating window or a friend's details had been open, PS Focus could stop showing hover highlights, clicks and scrolling until the mouse left it. Fixed, along with a rarer case where a drag could leave the app holding the mouse.
- **Transparency at your fingertips.** An icon at the left of the module icons opens a small slider beside it, and the window shows the change as you drag. The icon dims to match the setting. It has moved out of Settings.
- **Friend details, made easier.** Double-click anywhere on a friend's rows to see their hours and when they joined, and the next double-click always registers.
- **A proper scroll bar for Friends.** Drag it, or click its arrows or track; the list updates as you scroll.
- **A tidier rating window.** As wide as PS Focus, with stars that match the rest of the app and light up as you hover them.
- **Landscape keeps its width.** Coming back from Settings no longer leaves the window squashed narrow.
