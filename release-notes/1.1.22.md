## What's new

A quick update to **1.1.21**:

- **Fixed the app becoming unresponsive.** In rare cases, such as double-clicking a friend, PS Focus could stop reacting to hover and clicks until restarted. It now recovers on its own.
- **Transparency at your fingertips.** A new icon at the left of the module icons opens a small slider, and the window shows the change as you drag. The icon dims to match the setting. It has moved out of Settings.
- **A proper scroll bar for Friends.** Drag it, or click its arrows or track, and the list updates as you scroll.
- **Landscape keeps its width.** Coming back from Settings no longer leaves the window squashed narrow.

## Install

Download **PS-Focus-Setup-1.1.22.exe** below and run it. It updates an existing install and keeps your history, settings and Google sign-in.
