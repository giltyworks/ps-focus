## What it does

- **Tracks only real work.** Time counts while your art application is the window in front, and pauses after five minutes without input.
- **Shows your habits.** Day, week, month, and year charts; a calendar of your sessions; streaks and averages.
- **Levels from 1 to 99.** Your lifetime hours set your level, with a small celebration each time you reach a new one.
- **Stays out of the way.** It starts with Windows, sits in the tray, and needs no account.
- **Backs itself up.** Your history is saved on your PC with automatic backup copies. Connecting Google is optional and stores a backup in a private app folder in your own Google Drive.

## What it never records

PS Focus stores only how many seconds you worked, per application and hour. It does not record document names, window titles, keystrokes, screenshots, or file contents, and it has no advertising or analytics. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for the details.

## Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you connect, PS Focus asks for two things: access to its own hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive, where it keeps your settings and an activity backup, and your account email address, which it shows in the app. It cannot see any of your other Drive files, and nothing is sent to Giltyworks.

## Uninstalling

Open Windows **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**, choose PS Focus, then **Uninstall**. Your history is kept unless you tick **Also delete all user data**.
